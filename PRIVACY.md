# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

This privacy policy covers the personal publishing tool in this repository (ig-slides), including its Pinterest app.

## What the app does

The app is used only by its owner, Rakhmatullo Numonov, to publish the owner's own daily educational image carousels to the owner's own accounts (Instagram, Threads, Facebook and Pinterest).

## Data we access

- The owner's Pinterest account and board information, only to choose the board where the owner's own Pins are published.
- Access tokens issued by Pinterest for the owner's account.

The app does not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest user.

## How data is stored

Access tokens are stored as private environment variables on the server that runs the publishing task. They are never published in this repository or shared with anyone.

## Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

## Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest settings, which makes the stored tokens useless. Tokens are deleted when they are no longer needed.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue in this repository: https://github.com/numrakh/ig-slides/issues
